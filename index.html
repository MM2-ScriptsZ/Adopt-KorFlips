KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently asked questions

Everything about Korean servers & localization.

How do I connect to a Korean server?
When you launch Adopt Me, you'll automatically be routed to the best Korean server based on your location. You can manually select from 6 dedicated Korean servers in the server list menu.
Can I play with international friends on Korean servers?
Yes! Korean servers support cross-regional play. Your international friends can join you on Korean servers, though they may experience higher latency from their region.
Will events and updates be available on Korean servers?
Absolutely! All events, updates, and content are synchronized across all servers including Korean ones. You'll get the same experience as the global servers.
How to change game language to Korean?
Set your Roblox language preference to Korean in your account settings. When connected to a Korean server, the entire game UI will be localized to Korean automatically.
